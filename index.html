About

Connecting technical aviation research with public implementation.

I am a PhD candidate in Purdue University’s School of Aviation and Transportation Technology. My research connects aviation systems, transport policy, geographic information systems, and quantitative decision analysis.

I focus on questions that sit between technical feasibility and implementation: where emerging aviation systems can operate, under what safety conditions, and whether infrastructure choices create durable benefits for airports and metropolitan regions.

My background includes a BEng in Aeronautical/Aerospace Engineering, an MSc in Aviation Engineering, and professional experience in South Sudan’s civil aviation sector. That experience informs my interest in institutional capacity, safety disparities, and transport development.

Research agenda

Safe and sustainable aviation systems for complex operating environments.

My research examines emerging aviation systems from airport, metropolitan, and institutional perspectives.

01

Doctoral research

A GIS-based, scenario-driven framework for Advanced Air Mobility integration at U.S. large hub airports

The project screens candidate operating environments for safety feasibility, then evaluates viable scenarios across economic, operational, environmental, and social dimensions. The goal is to produce a transparent decision-support framework for airports, regulators, and metropolitan planning institutions.
